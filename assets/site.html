BEHIND THE MARKS

Rain is a number.

Download vector artwork ↗
01

One day, one mark.

Read months downward and days across. Blue strokes descend with rainfall; the orange stroke marks 5 August, the highest daily reading. Open circles preserve trace rainfall as a category, rather than inventing an exact value.

02

Two scales, the same rain.

The square-root scale reveals light rain alongside the largest downpours. Switch to linear to see lengths proportional to millimetres. Both use the same 0–400 mm range in every month. Monthly totals always remain unchanged.

03

What a score cannot tell you.

One station cannot describe every district. Daily totals hide when rain fell and how intense it was within the day. The concentration in one year does not establish a long-term climate trend.
